# Computational-Graphics-and-Visualization

Throughout this course, I learned how to create 3D objects and implement camera movement to explore the scene.

**1. How do I approach designing software?**
   
  I approach design by breaking it down into smaller components. For this specific project, I analyzed the 3D image and recreated it from the ground up. I focused on making the base and tray first as a start before focusing on the objects themselves, making the more difficult ones first. Then I sourced textures and finally added lighting, going back to adjust any changes for the final look.

-What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

  I developed skills in translating physical objects into 3D space using basic meshes, textures, materials, and light sources. I learned how design choices—such as scale, color, and surface reflection—affect both realism and scene readability.

-What design process did you follow for your project work?

  My process included mapping out each object and building the scene piece by piece. Then deciding how to build each part from OpenGL’s library of primitive meshes.
  
-How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

  For future work, the ability to plan visually, break down complex ideas, and document design decisions will help in programming tasks such as UI design, 3D modeling, and any project that benefits from modular planning.
  

2. How do I approach developing programs?
   
  I approach development by focusing on getting a basic version working first and then iterating. I test frequently and improve functionality step by step rather than trying to complete everything at once. This helps in reducing bugs and not feeling overwhelmed in trying to focus on many things at once. 

-What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

  I learned how to manage shader programs, material properties, and texture mapping. I also had trial-and-error testing to fine-tune lighting and spatial layout, which helped deepen my debugging skills.
  
-How did iteration factor into your development?

  I continuously reviwed the scene and made incremental adjustments, improving accuracy over time. Each milestone represented a more refined version of the previous work.

-How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?

  Early on, I focused on simply getting objects to render, but by the final milestones, I understood how to structure code, reuse meshes, adjust lighting, and apply textures.

3. How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
   
  Computer science strengthens my problem-solving skills and opens up career paths in software development, data, graphics, and beyond. This project pushed me to think creatively and technically at the same time while getting used to iterating more and adjusting values constantly to recreate the results I desired. It has also helped me learn a field I was always interested in and never knew how to get into.

  I now have hands-on experience with rendering pipelines, lighting models, shaders, and 3D math. I can build on these in future courses, such as advanced graphics, VR/AR, or game development.
  
-How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?

  These skills apply to careers involving visualization, simulation, scientific research tools, UX design, and 3D game engines. Knowing how visual experiences are built helps me understand how software can bring creative ideas to life.
